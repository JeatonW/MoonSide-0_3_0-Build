Controls:

ESC: Settings menu
WASD: Move
L: Accept (in menu) OR Dialogue Box (currently disabled)
Semicolon: Back (in menu) OR Battle HUD
Apostrophe: Open or completely close menu
P: Main menu state preview

*** IMPORTANT NOTES:
